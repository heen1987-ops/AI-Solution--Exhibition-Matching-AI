## Public Interface

The kiosk endpoints intentionally have no profile/auth headers and accept only:

- `kiosk_device_id`
- `language`
- `guest_session_id`

They do not accept or return name, phone, email, address, login ID, or other PII.

## Behavior

- `POST /kiosk/sessions` requires an `ACTIVE` `exhibition.kiosk_device`.
- The created session is stored in `profile.guest_session` with
  `entry_channel = 'KIOSK'`, `device_type = 'KIOSK'`, and the requested language.
- Session expiry uses `exhibition.kiosk_config.idle_timeout_seconds` when present,
  otherwise the backend default of 90 seconds.
- `DELETE /kiosk/sessions/{guest_session_id}` is idempotent. Existing sessions are
  expired immediately and any QR `entry_code` is cleared.
- `POST /kiosk/qr-sessions` reuses `profile.guest_session.entry_code` and creates no
  new table.
- Missing, expired, or non-kiosk sessions return `QR_HANDOFF_EXPIRED`.

## Guardrails

- No kiosk signup, login, contact form, or persistent visitor profile was added.
- Inactive, maintenance, retired, and missing kiosk devices are hidden behind
  `RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND`.
- QR handoff does not embed personal data; it returns only `entry_code` and
  `expires_at`.
- No precise indoor location, real-time crowd, inventory, CRM, contracts, payments,
  or automatic learning scope was added.

## Known Limitations

QR consumption and guest-to-registered profile conversion are not implemented in this
task. That belongs to `BAC-007`. Until then, `entry_code` is issued and stored, but
single-use consumption must be enforced by the future conversion endpoint.

DB-backed integration tests are not added here because `backend/tests/**` is
QA_SECURITY owned. A QA test request was created.
